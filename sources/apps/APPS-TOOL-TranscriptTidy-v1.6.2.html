        Help

        1. Drop AI Studio JSON files.
        2. Add or skip each card.
        3. Save .md (zip if many), or Copy.

        Privacy

        Nothing is uploaded. Only your look is remembered on this device. Reload clears your files. Saved files are your only copies.

        Clear

        Clear empties the list. Undo brings it back until reload.

        Limits

        20 MB per file. 100 MB in review. 1,000 chats per file. Odd JSON is refused and listed under Errors.

        Formats

        AI Studio chat, generic chat (messages with role and content), and experimental ChatGPT-style trees. Change a card's format with its menu. Try real exports on a copy first.

        Same input gives the same output. No clock time is written.
