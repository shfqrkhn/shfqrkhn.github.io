            Help

            Where is my data?
            In this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing site data erases it, so export now and then.
            Backup
            Export JSON or ZIP restores everything. Markdown and Print are for reading. Files are not encrypted.
            Delete
            Fix and storage, then Reset local data. It downloads a backup first.
            Ranking
            Ratings are your own judgement. Single rating: sorted by rating. Weighted: score is the sum of weight share times rating. Empty means not rated, never zero. A lead under 0.5 points is a close call (this app's own rule of thumb).
            Convert
            Convert to weighted matrix keeps every rating as one criterion, Overall rating, and keeps a copy of the old state. Press twice.
            What would change the winner
            For each criterion, the smallest weight change at which another option ties the leader.
            Do nothing
            Adds the option of changing nothing, for comparison.
            Journal
            Undoable decisions need light care. Hard-to-undo ones need full care: do nothing listed, 3 weighted criteria, 2 pieces of evidence, a pre-mortem, a clear lead, an expected result, a review date. The pre-mortem is a published decision-research technique. After the review date a reminder appears until you record the result.
            Templates
            Example criteria and weights written for this app. Change them freely.
            Update
            Replace the file. Saved data stays. Templates are in the block named ledger-content, colours in :root. Content version 1, 2026-10-31.
